### 🛠️ **Pioneer Deepworks – Company Lore & Origin**

**Founded:** 2127 CE  
**Headquarters:** Neo-Toronto Arcology, Earth  
**Slogan:** _“We go where bedrock breaks.”_

---

### 🌌 **Overview**

**Pioneer Deepworks** began as a high-risk, high-reward deep crustal extraction firm during Earth’s Great Resource Crisis. Initially focused on uninhabitable zones like the Siberian Fault Rift and Mars’ Valles Marineris basin, the company quickly earned a reputation for deploying into **the most volatile and inaccessible locations** with alarming success.

By the mid-22nd century, they pioneered **autonomous gravity-bore rigs**, atmospheric vacuum chambers, and “drill-thru” slipgates — technology that opened the door to mining on moons, asteroids, and **xeno-terraforming zones**.

---

### 🧱 **Corporate Culture**

Pioneer prides itself on a “no questions, no hesitation” culture. 

Publicly praised for their innovation and bravery and very respectful of “The Return Gap”.

---


### 🛰️ **Current Flagship Projects**

- **Black Vein Initiative (Proxima-B):** Deep-core xeno-mineral shaft suspected to contain psion-reactive crystal.
- **Driftwake Excavation (Interstellar Anomaly):** Reverse-engineering gravitational fluctuations that simulate infinite geological depth.
- And **many others** non-disclosed projects (purely for monopoly protection and security reasons evidently).
