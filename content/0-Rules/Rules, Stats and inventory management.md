### Advantage/Disadvantage
Rolling with advantage : roll twice take the highest score.
Rolling with disadvantage: roll twice take the lowest score.

NB when an enemy is at #melee range <5ft, #Mid-range and #Long-range weapons must be shot with disadvantage

### Proficiency
Add the number to the appropriate hit roll.

### Heroic Inspiration

Sometimes the DM gives you Heroic Inspiration. If you have Heroic Inspiration, you can expend it to reroll any die immediately after rolling it, and you must use the new roll.

**_Only One at a Time._** You can never have more than one instance of Heroic Inspiration. If something gives you Heroic Inspiration and you already have it, you can give it to a player character in your group who lacks it.

**_Gaining Heroic Inspiration._** Your DM can give you Heroic Inspiration for a variety of reasons. Typically, DMs award it when you do something **particularly heroic, in character, or entertaining**. It's a reward for making the game more fun for everyone playing.

### Pioneer Depworks perks
The company always monitors their worker for their safety (what else, huh?).

Multiple perks can be stored in your data at the same time.
### Unconscious
When falling to 0 HP, become [[Unconscious]].
If your character gets damaged 2 consecutive rounds while unconcious, he dies.
Damage over time (DoT) is not removed when unconscious and counts as a damage.
Can be saved by having a [[Stabilisator Gel]] used on him or other similar actions/traits.

### Weapons
You can have at anytime 1 #One-handed weapon and 1 #Two-handed weapon.
The slot for the #Two-handed weapon can be used for 2 #One-handed instead.

### Range
The range written in the stats indicates the maximum range at which you can attack without disadvantage. The #short-range or #melee range disadavantage can still apply for certain weapons

### Inventory
The presence of an item in the inventory, or character sheet in general, allows the usage.
If it is a #consumable, delete it from the inventory.

### Clips
Only one clip is shown in the weapon sheet, but 3 of them are available at any time to the player (unless explicitely written).

Use the method of your choice to track your clips (filled or empty) and types of clips.
Use the method of your choice to count the number of bullets used or charges expanded in the weapon sheet.

### Mining Credits
The Miner's guild credits is the currency used when working outside of Earth's sector.
It can be converted to the money currency of your choice upon return accordingly to the exchange rate.

Mining credits are used to replenish the inventory, buy accessories, upgrades and ammunition.
Complete Pioneer Deepworks bounties to get more credits or try to find some in human settlements.

No refunds! Keep your weapons in a cyber stash or drop them, but Pioneer Deepworks will not buying back used property.

### Armor class (AC)
Check the stats of your currently equipped armor to determine your AC.

### Traits
Traits are special action unique to your role in Pioneer Deepworks.

### [[Available Actions]]
Action can be attacks depending on your weapons selected or traits and can always be combined with movements according to your movement speed.

### Reactions
Reactions are bonus actions in response to attacks or other contexts.

You can make an Opportunity Attack when a creature that you can see leaves your immediate reach (5 ft) using its action, its Bonus Action, its Reaction, or one of its specials traits or is moved by a third party. To make the Opportunity Attack, take a Reaction to make one #melee attack with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike against the provoking creature. The attack occurs right before the creature leaves your reach.

**_Avoiding Opportunity Attack._** You can avoid provoking an Opportunity Attack by taking the Disengage action. 

### Bonus actions
A bonus action can always be used in complement to the main action, but it must be categorized as one.

### Skills and Saving throws
Are the only modifiers available for checks, no other bonuses are awarded for other categories.
In all cases roll 1d20 and add any modifiers and/or bonus

### Critical rolls
Unless stated, a critical hit is obtained by rolling a natural 20.
Unless stated a critical fail is obtained by rolling a natural 1.

### Short rest
A short rest (1h) cannot be taken less than 4 in-game hours appart. 
Short rests are prioritized while in a mission.
Rests the used traits or tools appropriately.
Heal 2d6+1.

### Long Rest
A long rest (6 hours of sleep and no more than 2 hours of light activity, such as reading, talking, eating, or standing watch.) 
Cannot be taken less than 12 in-game hours appart or when enemies are in close proximity.
Resets the used traits or tools appropriately.
Recover max Health.
