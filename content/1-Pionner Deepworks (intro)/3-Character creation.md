#rules #intro
Think of this adventure like a Rainbow Six Siege/Dead Space/DnD/etc crossover.
- It's survival based, more realistic as you need to eat and drink (in a sci-fi setting) 
- with intense inventory management (without encumberment, but slots) 
- and a quick pace (some things can happen or not if a decision takes too much time).

It's more story/task/objective/mission driven, as a video game would be, if you don't move in the maps or tell specific actions you want to do, progress will be pretty much halted. 

Knowing well your sheets and tools will help greatly. The SM (Space Master/DM) will not help more than necessary.

You can still question the SM on mechanics and mix possibilities. The SM is always right on a decision that is needed in those contexts.

As it is something completely invented, some exploits, gray zones, missing links or spelling errors might be present in the various documents that will be mitigated following the SM's decision. 

There are predetermined locations for encounters, loot and story development, also secrets/information to discover/infer/uncover **that you may miss.** 
Many mechanics are different from a classic DnD setting: be alert!
